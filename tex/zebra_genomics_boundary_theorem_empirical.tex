% Measure-theoretic convention for the theory core.  This makes explicit the
% regularity required by the Radon--Nikodym identities used in the proof.
\newcommand{\theoryregularitynote}{%
\par\noindent\textit{Regularity convention.}
Throughout the likelihood-ratio arguments, the case laws are taken to be absolutely continuous with respect to the corresponding control laws, so the likelihood ratios are Radon--Nikodym derivatives and satisfy $\Ezero[\LR]=1$. Where A1 invokes a two-sided conditional genomic bound, the corresponding conditional laws are mutually absolutely continuous and the bounds are interpreted $\Pzero$-almost surely. A2 is an essential-unboundedness statement on the control support. Statements involving LR$+$ presume positive false-positive rate.
\par
}

\newcommand{\theoryassumptionnote}{%
\par\noindent\textit{Role of the two sides of A1.}
The upper bound $M$ alone gives the ``no upward rescue'' statement below $\lambda/M$; the lower bound $m>0$ supplies the corresponding ``no downward reversal'' statement above $\lambda/m$ and the two-sided $O(1)$ log-tail equivalence in part~(v). The genomic-only power bound additionally uses the separate marginal upper bound $M_G$.
\par
}
