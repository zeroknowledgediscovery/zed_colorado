# ZeBRA--genomics manuscript

This directory contains the canonical theory + empirical manuscript for the ZeBRA--genomics likelihood-ratio paper.

## Canonical build

The single canonical document is:

```text
zebra_genomics_boundary_theorem_empirical.tex
```

Build it with:

```bash
cd tex
./build_full_manuscript.sh
```

The resulting PDF contains both the main manuscript and the complete Supplementary Material at the back. There is no separate supplementary document.

The canonical narrative is modular and uses the external BibTeX bibliography `zebra_genomics_references.bib` with the standard `IEEEtran` bibliography style. The back-of-document Supplementary Material contains the auxiliary proofs and the full robustness/diagnostic analyses.

The older `zebra_genomics_boundary_theorem.tex`, `empirical_section_update.tex`, and bundled historical drafts are not part of the canonical build.

## Empirical scope

The current manuscript is intentionally restricted to one phenotype:

```text
FILD or FILA ADJUDICATED
```

The two sparse nonfibrotic endpoints explored during analysis development are not part of the current manuscript argument. The biological genomic focus is the MUC5B rs35705950 channel, with the broader selected genomic panel used for global incremental-prediction analyses.

## Figure hierarchy: exactly two integrated empirical figures

The current publication layout uses one empirical figure in the main manuscript and one empirical figure in the Supplementary Material. This is intentional: future ADRD/APOE4 and HFrEF/TTNtv analyses can use the same panel architecture as disease-specific integrated figures.

### Main Figure 1: integrated FILD/FILA evidence

`\figIntegratedFILD` contains eight panels:

1. FILD/FILA held-out AUC for ZeBRA, genomics, and the nonlinear combined learner;
2. paired incremental FILD/FILA AUC after adding the complete genomic panel to ZeBRA;
3. repeated-split SHAP recurrence showing ZeBRA as the dominant feature and rs35705950 MUC5B as the leading recurrent genomic feature;
4. ZeBRA-to-MUC5B carrier-prediction AUC, showing that ZeBRA does not reconstruct genotype;
5. local directional nested-model LR/deviance statistics across ZeBRA-defined control-FPR bands;
6. empirical residual-genomic high-probability scales `bhat_delta` across the nine prespecified direct-LR estimators;
7. the actual cross-fitted clinical `Lambda_Z` and residual `Lambda_{G|Z}` evidence channels along the ZeBRA axis; and
8. localization of genomic-induced decision flips around the clinical likelihood-ratio boundary.

This one figure is intended to carry the complete empirical theorem-data argument: no reproducible global genomic increment, genuine use of MUC5B by the combined learner, distinct clinical/genomic channels, localized residual genomic information, direct LR factorization, finite residual scale, and boundary-localized decision changes.

### Supplementary Figure S1: integrated extended diagnostics

`\figIntegratedSupplementary` contains eight panels:

1. local adjusted ZeBRA and MUC5B effect-size trajectories;
2. robustness of upper-tail clinical evidence exceeding the measured residual-genomic scale;
3. matched-FPR rescue sensitivity gain;
4. corresponding operational FPR change;
5. stringent held-out LR+ across ZeBRA, genomic, and combined models;
6. corresponding stringent held-out sensitivity;
7. explicitly adjudicated-negative rescue-minus-matched FPR diagnostic; and
8. supporting ROC-envelope/hull AUC quantities, clearly labeled as descriptive geometry rather than classifier performance.

Calibration and complete numerical summaries remain in Supplementary Tables because those values are more concise and less ambiguous in tabular form.

## Figure implementation

The figures are generated with PGFPlots/TikZ at compile time. `empirical_figures.tex` loads:

```text
empirical_figures_data.tex
empirical_figures_primary.tex
empirical_figures_lr.tex
empirical_figures_integrated.tex
```

The first file defines the canonical result tables and compact extracted quantities. The two historical figure-definition files are retained only as source-level provenance and are not called by the current manuscript. The publication figures themselves are defined in `empirical_figures_integrated.tex`.

All plotted empirical values are read from the canonical analysis results under `../zebra_comp/RESULTS`, except for explicitly documented repeated-split SHAP recurrence counts and the supplementary ROC-hull summary values. The actual cross-fitted LR channel curves are read from the moving-window summaries generated by the direct likelihood-ratio analysis.

## Theory hierarchy

The central theorem is finite-stage boundary localization. At a fixed clinical-history stage, the finite-stage stochastic residual scale is sufficient for the high-probability boundary-localization result; neither A1-U nor A2 is required.

A1-U and A2 are introduced only for the separate conditional sequential tail-extension theorem. A1-U requires a residual-genomic bound uniform over clinical-history length, while A2 assumes that arbitrarily strong positive clinical evidence is attainable on rare trajectories. The Colorado analysis does not prove either assumption.

The empirical analysis instantiates the exact observable score-level factorization

```text
Lambda_Z,G = Lambda_Z * Lambda_G|Z
```

and estimates the score-level empirical residual scale. It does not establish full-history sufficiency, a universal genome-wide bound, or mathematical unboundedness of clinical evidence.

## Supplementary Material

The Supplementary Material is included after the conclusion in the same PDF. In addition to Supplementary Figure S1, it contains:

- proofs of auxiliary theoretical results;
- detailed repeated-split SHAP stability;
- direct-LR estimation details and complete numerical sensitivity summaries;
- local diagnostic p-values and selected moving-window values;
- matched-FPR MUC5B rescue and adjudicated-negative numerical tables;
- calibration diagnostics;
- the supporting ROC-hull interpretation; and
- explicit scope statements for the empirical likelihood-ratio result.

It is written as scientific supplementary material and does not enumerate computational result filenames or repository provenance paths.
