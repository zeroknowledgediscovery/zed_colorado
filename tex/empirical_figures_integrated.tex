\caption{\textbf{Integrated FILD/FILA evidence for boundary-localized genomic utility.}
(a) Mean held-out AUC across 30 repeated outer splits: ZeBRA dominates globally, while the selected genomic panel is weak and the nonlinear combined learner does not improve on ZeBRA. (b) In the paired regularized comparison on the same held-out subjects, adding the full genomic panel changes AUC by essentially zero; whiskers show the split-wise 2.5th--97.5th percentile range. (c) The combined learner nevertheless uses genomic information: ZeBRA is the top SHAP feature in every split, while rs35705950 \textit{MUC5B} is the most recurrent genomic feature (28/30 top-20 appearances). (d) ZeBRA predicts \textit{MUC5B} T-carrier status at chance level in pooled and disease-stratified analyses, excluding genotype reconstruction as the explanation for weak global genomic increment; carrier prevalence is nevertheless substantially higher in FILD/FILA cases than adjudicated negatives. (e) Residual \textit{MUC5B} contribution after ZeBRA is heterogeneous across clinically relevant score bands and exceeds the converse nested-model contribution in each displayed band. (f) The control-side residual-genomic scale is stable at the 95\% and 99\% levels across nine prespecified LR estimators; the 99.5\% tail is more estimator sensitive. (g) The actual cross-fitted score-level evidence channels show a clinical log-LR that rises strongly with ZeBRA state while carrier and non-carrier residual \textit{MUC5B} effects remain on a smaller, state-dependent scale. (h) Genomic-induced decision changes remain concentrated near the clinical likelihood-ratio boundary across stringent operating points. Together the panels show why a biologically strong genomic channel can remain locally decision-relevant while contributing essentially no global discrimination beyond a strong longitudinal clinical-history score.}
\label{fig:integrated-fild}

\caption{\textbf{Integrated supplementary diagnostics for the FILD/FILA analysis.}

(b) Across the nine prespecified direct-LR estimators, clinical log-LR increasingly exceeds the measured statewise residual-genomic scale in the upper ZeBRA tail; points are medians and whiskers span the estimator range.
